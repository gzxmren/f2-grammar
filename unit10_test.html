Unit 10: Expressions + -ing — Practice Test

總分：100分 | 時限：40分鐘

Section A: MC 10題（每題3分）

1. It's no use ______ with him. He won't change his mind.

✅ 答案：B

2. There ______ a lot of tourists coming for the festival.

✅ 答案：B

3. She is busy ______ for her exam.

✅ 答案：B

4. I had difficulty ______ a taxi in the rain.

✅ 答案：B

5. It's not worth ______ about such small things.

✅ 答案：B

6. He spent the whole afternoon ______ video games.

✅ 答案：B

7. It's a waste of time ______ that movie.

✅ 答案：B

8. She is used to ______ up early in the morning.

✅ 答案：B

9. Let's go ______ this weekend!

✅ 答案：B

10. I have difficulty ______ myself in English.

✅ 答案：B

Section B: Fill in 10題（每題3分）

11. It's no use _______________ (argue) with him.

✅ 答案：arguing

12. There were some workmen _______________ (take down) the scaffolding.

✅ 答案：taking down

13. She is busy _______________ (prepare) for her exam.

✅ 答案：preparing

14. I had difficulty _______________ (find) a taxi.

✅ 答案：finding

15. It's worth _______________ (visit) the museum.

✅ 答案：visiting

16. He spent hours _______________ (try) to fix the computer.

✅ 答案：trying

17. Don't waste time _______________ (play) video games.

✅ 答案：playing

18. She is used to _______________ (live) in a big city.

✅ 答案：living

19. It didn't take long to get used to _______________ (drive) on the left.

✅ 答案：driving

20. Let's go _______________ (shop) this afternoon.

✅ 答案：shopping

Section C: Error Correction 5題（每題4分）

21. It's no use to argue with him.

✅ It's no use arguing with him.

22. I have difficulty to express myself in English.

✅ I have difficulty expressing myself in English.

23. She is busy to prepare for her exam.

✅ She is busy preparing for her exam.

24. It's not worth to worry about it.

✅ It's not worth worrying about it.

25. I am used to wake up early.

✅ I am used to waking up early.

Section D: Rewriting 5題（每題4分）

26. It is useless to argue with him. (用 no use) → It's _______________

✅ 答案：no use arguing with him

27. She is busy. She is preparing for the test. (合併) → She is busy _______________

✅ 答案：preparing for the test

28. I find it difficult to express myself. (用 have difficulty) → I have difficulty _______________

✅ 答案：expressing myself

29. He wasted money. He bought cheap products. (合併) → He wasted money _______________

✅ 答案：buying cheap products

30. She now finds it normal to live here. (用 used to) → She is used to _______________

✅ 答案：living here
