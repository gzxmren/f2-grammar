Unit 11: Bare Infinitive — Practice Test

總分：100分 | 時限：40分鐘

Section A: MC 10題（每題3分）

1. Let's ______ for a swim!

✅ 答案：A

2. Why don't we ______ a movie tonight?

✅ 答案：A

3. I saw him ______ the bus and walk into the shop.

✅ 答案：A

4. I heard someone ______ in the next room.

✅ 答案：B

5. All I did was ______ more water.

✅ 答案：D

6. The only thing you can do is ______ for their call.

✅ 答案：D

7. I'd like to go and ______ some snacks.

✅ 答案：A

8. Why not ______ again?

✅ 答案：A

9. We watched the sun ______ below the horizon.

✅ 答案：A

10. He wanted nothing but ______ his guitar.

✅ 答案：A

Section B: Fill in 10題（每題3分）

11. Let's _______________ (go) for a walk.

✅ 答案：go

12. Why don't we _______________ (watch) a movie?

✅ 答案：watch

13. I saw her _______________ (cross) the road and enter the building.

✅ 答案：cross

14. I heard someone _______________ (sing) in the next room.

✅ 答案：singing

15. All I did was _______________ (wait) for you.

✅ 答案：wait || to wait

16. The only thing you can do is _______________ (hope).

✅ 答案：hope || to hope

17. Why not _______________ (try) again?

✅ 答案：try

18. I'd like to sit down and _______________ (rest).

✅ 答案：rest

19. We could do nothing except _______________ (wait).

✅ 答案：wait

20. He saw the children _______________ (play) in the park when he passed by.

✅ 答案：playing

Section C: Error Correction 5題（每題4分）

21. Let's to go for a swim.

✅ Let's go for a swim.

22. I saw him to get off the bus.

✅ I saw him get off the bus.

23. Why don't we to watch a movie?

✅ Why don't we watch a movie?

24. All I did was to waiting for you.

✅ All I did was (to) wait for you.

25. He wanted nothing but to playing his guitar.

✅ He wanted nothing but play his guitar.

Section D: Rewriting 5題（每題4分）

26. Shall we go for a walk? (用 Let's) → _______________

✅ 答案：Let's go for a walk

27. You should try again. (用 Why not) → _______________

✅ 答案：Why not try again

28. I watched the process: the sun set below the horizon. (用 bare inf) → I watched the sun _______________

✅ 答案：set below the horizon

29. I only waited for you, nothing else. (用 All I did) → All I did _______________

✅ 答案：was (to) wait for you

30. You can only wait. (用 The only thing) → The only thing _______________

✅ 答案：you can do is (to) wait
