Unit 12: Conjunctions — Practice Test

總分：100分 | 時限：40分鐘

Section A: MC 10題（每題3分）

1. ______ it rained, we enjoyed the picnic.

✅ 答案：B

2. She stayed home ______ she was sick.

✅ 答案：B

3. He left early ______ he wouldn't miss the bus.

✅ 答案：A

4. Tom likes sports ______ Jerry prefers reading.

✅ 答案：B

5. The box was ______ heavy that I couldn't lift it.

✅ 答案：B

6. It was ______ a heavy box that I couldn't lift it.

✅ 答案：B

7. ______ the rain, they continued playing.

✅ 答案：B

8. She worked hard ______ she could pass the exam.

✅ 答案：A

9. You're tired. ______, let's take a break.

✅ 答案：C

10. ______ you're tired, let's rest.

✅ 答案：C

Section B: Fill in 10題（每題3分）

11. _______________ (雖然) it rained, we enjoyed the picnic.

✅ 答案：Although || Though || Even though

12. She stayed home _______________ (因為) her illness.

✅ 答案：because of

13. I left early _______________ (為了) I wouldn't miss the bus.

✅ 答案：so that

14. Tom likes sports _______________ (而) Jerry prefers reading.

✅ 答案：while || whereas

15. The box was _______________ heavy _______________ I couldn't lift it.

✅ 答案：so; that

16. It was _______________ a heavy box _______________ I couldn't lift it.

✅ 答案：such; that

17. _______________ (儘管) the rain, they continued playing.

✅ 答案：Despite || In spite of

18. She worked hard _______________ (為了) pass the exam.

✅ 答案：in order to

19. _______________ (既然) you're tired, let's rest.

✅ 答案：Since || As

20. It rained. _______________ (然而), we enjoyed the picnic.

✅ 答案：However || Nevertheless

Section C: Error Correction 5題（每題4分）

21. Despite it rained, we enjoyed the picnic.

✅ Although it rained, we enjoyed the picnic. / Despite the rain, we enjoyed the picnic.

22. She stayed home because of she was sick.

✅ She stayed home because she was sick.

23. The box was such heavy that I couldn't lift it.

✅ The box was so heavy that I couldn't lift it.

24. It was so a heavy box that I couldn't lift it.

✅ It was such a heavy box that I couldn't lift it.

25. He left early for not missing the bus.

✅ He left early so that he wouldn't miss the bus.

Section D: Rewriting 5題（每題4分）

26. We enjoyed the picnic even though it rained. (用 despite) → _______________

✅ 答案：We enjoyed the picnic despite the rain.

27. Because she was sick, she stayed home. (用 because of) → _______________

✅ 答案：She stayed home because of her sickness.

28. The coffee is very hot. I cannot drink it. (用 so...that) → _______________

✅ 答案：The coffee is so hot that I cannot drink it.

29. He studied hard because he wanted to pass the exam. (用 so that) → _______________

✅ 答案：He studied hard so that he could pass the exam.

30. She left early. She wanted to catch the first train. (用 in order to) → _______________

✅ 答案：She left early in order to catch the first train.
