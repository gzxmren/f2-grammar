Unit 13: Quantifiers with Relative Pronouns（數量詞 + 關係代詞）

本單元學習 how many/much, some of which/whom 等數量詞與關係代詞的搭配。

13.1 Quantifier + of + whom/which

在關係從句中，可用「數量詞 + of + whom/which」來修飾人或事物。

數量詞例句
some of whom/whichShe has painted a lot of paintings, some of which are very famous.
many of whom/whichHis cousins, many of whom he had never met, were all at the party.
a few of whom/whichShe has several friends, a few of whom are from Japan.
most of whom/whichThe students, most of whom had studied hard, passed the exam.
all of whom/whichI have three brothers, all of whom are taller than me.
none of whom/whichThe answers, none of which were correct, disappointed the teacher.
both of whom/whichShe has two daughters, both of whom are doctors.
neither of whom/whichHe suggested two plans, neither of which was feasible.
each of whom/whichThe team members, each of whom contributed a lot, were praised.

13.2 Preposition + whom/which

介詞可以放在關係代詞 whom/which 前面（正式用法）。

  • The person to whom you spoke is the manager.
  • The company for which she works is based in London.
  • The topic about which we talked was very interesting.

13.3 whose（所有格關係代詞）

whose 表示「⋯⋯的」，可指人或物。

  • I know a girl whose father is a famous singer.
  • That's the house whose roof was damaged in the storm.

考核要點 ✅

  1. some/many/most/all/none/both/neither/each + of + whom/which
  2. 介詞 + whom/which 的正式用法
  3. whose 表「⋯⋯的」，可指人或物
