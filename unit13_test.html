Unit 13: Quantifiers & Relative Pronouns — Test

總分：100分 | 時限：40分鐘

Section A: MC 10題（每題3分）

1. She has three brothers, all of ______ are taller than her.

✅ 答案：B

2. He has many paintings, some of ______ are very valuable.

✅ 答案：C

3. I know a girl ______ father is a famous singer.

✅ 答案：C

4. The person to ______ you spoke is the manager.

✅ 答案：B

5. She has two daughters, both of ______ are doctors.

✅ 答案：B

6. I have several friends, a few of ______ are from Japan.

✅ 答案：B

7. The answers, none of ______ were correct, disappointed the teacher.

✅ 答案：C

8. He suggested two plans, neither of ______ was feasible.

✅ 答案：C

9. The company for ______ she works is based in London.

✅ 答案：C

10. That's the house ______ roof was damaged in the storm.

✅ 答案：C

Section B: Fill in 10題（每題3分）

11. She has three brothers, all of _______________ are taller than her.

✅ 答案：whom

12. He has many paintings, some of _______________ are very valuable.

✅ 答案：which

13. I know a girl _______________ father is a famous singer.

✅ 答案：whose

14. The person to _______________ you spoke is the manager.

✅ 答案：whom

15. She has two daughters, both of _______________ are doctors.

✅ 答案：whom

16. The students, most of _______________ studied hard, passed.

✅ 答案：whom

17. He suggested two plans, neither of _______________ was feasible.

✅ 答案：which

18. That's the house _______________ roof was damaged.

✅ 答案：whose

19. The topic about _______________ we talked was interesting.

✅ 答案：which

20. I have three books, all of _______________ are borrowed from the library.

✅ 答案：which

Section C: Error Correction 5題（每題4分）

21. She has three brothers, all of who are taller than her.

✅ all of whom are taller than her.

22. I know a girl who father is a famous singer.

✅ whose father is a famous singer.

23. The person to who you spoke is the manager.

✅ to whom you spoke is the manager.

24. The company for which she works is based in London.

✅ No error

25. He suggested two plans, neither of them was feasible.

✅ neither of which was feasible.

Section D: Rewriting 5題（每題4分）

26. She has three brothers. All of them are taller than her. (合併) → She has three brothers, _______________

✅ 答案：all of whom are taller than her

27. The topic was interesting. We talked about it. (用 about which) → The topic _______________

✅ 答案：about which we talked was interesting

28. I know a girl. Her father is a singer. (用 whose) → I know a girl _______________

✅ 答案：whose father is a singer

29. He has many paintings. Some of them are valuable. (合併) → He has many paintings, _______________

✅ 答案：some of which are valuable

30. She has two plans. Neither plan was feasible. (用 neither of which) → She has two plans, _______________

✅ 答案：neither of which was feasible
