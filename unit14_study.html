Unit 14: Adjective + Preposition（形容詞 + 介詞）

本單元學習形容詞與特定介詞的搭配用法。

14.1 常見形容詞 + 介詞搭配

形容詞 + 介詞用法例句
fond of喜歡Children are fond of ice-cream.
keen on熱衷於I'm not very keen on football.
crazy about狂熱於She's crazy about dancing.
good at擅長Keira is good at cycling.
good for對⋯有益Swimming is good for you.
good to對⋯友善Mrs Wong is good to every student.
angry at/with對某人生氣The customer was angry at/with the salesperson.
angry at/about對某事生氣She was angry about the delay.
happy about/with對某事滿意He was happy about/with his results.
happy for為某人高興We are happy for you.
disappointed about/at/by/with對某事失望She was disappointed about the results.
disappointed in/with對某人失望I'm disappointed in you.

14.2 nice/kind/good/friendly/rude/polite + to

描述對待某人的行為態度時用 to。

  • They were very nice to us.
  • She is always polite to her teachers.
  • He was rude to the waiter.

14.3 for + 形容詞（說明原因）

用 for 說明某種情緒的原因。

  • She was angry with him for being late.
  • I'm grateful for your help.
  • He is famous for his novels.

考核要點 ✅

  1. fond of / keen on / crazy about（喜好）
  2. good at（擅長）/ good for（有益）/ good to（友善）
  3. angry at/with + 人；angry at/about + 事
  4. happy about/with + 事；happy for + 人
  5. disappointed about/at/by/with + 事；disappointed in/with + 人
  6. nice/kind/rude/polite + to someone
  7. for + 名詞/-ing 說明原因
