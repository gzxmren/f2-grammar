Unit 14: Adjective + Preposition — Test

總分：100分 | 時限：40分鐘

Section A: MC 10題（每題3分）

1. Children are fond ______ ice-cream.

✅ 答案：A

2. She's crazy ______ dancing.

✅ 答案：B

3. Keira is good ______ cycling.

✅ 答案：B

4. Swimming is good ______ your health.

✅ 答案：C

5. The customer was angry ______ the salesperson.

✅ 答案：C

6. I'm not very keen ______ football.

✅ 答案：B

7. We are happy ______ your success.

✅ 答案：C

8. She was disappointed ______ her exam results.

✅ 答案：C

9. The teacher is always polite ______ her students.

✅ 答案：B

10. He is famous ______ his novels.

✅ 答案：C

Section B: Fill in 10題（每題3分）

11. Children are fond _______________ ice-cream.

✅ 答案：of

12. She's crazy _______________ dancing.

✅ 答案：about

13. Keira is good _______________ cycling.

✅ 答案：at

14. Swimming is good _______________ your health.

✅ 答案：for

15. I'm not very keen _______________ football.

✅ 答案：on

16. The customer was angry _______________ the salesperson.

✅ 答案：at || with

17. She was angry _______________ the delay.

✅ 答案：about || at

18. We are happy _______________ your success.

✅ 答案：about || with

19. He was rude _______________ the waiter.

✅ 答案：to

20. She was disappointed _______________ her son's behaviour.

✅ 答案：in || with

Section C: Error Correction 5題（每題4分）

21. Children are fond with ice-cream.

✅ fond of ice-cream

22. She's crazy for dancing.

✅ crazy about dancing

23. Keira is good for cycling (擅長).

✅ good at cycling

24. He was angry with the delay (對事).

✅ angry about/at the delay

25. He was rude with the waiter.

✅ rude to the waiter

Section D: Rewriting 5題（每題4分）

26. I like ice-cream a lot. (用 fond) → I am _______________

✅ 答案：fond of ice-cream

27. She is very interested in dancing. (用 crazy) → She is _______________

✅ 答案：crazy about dancing

28. He plays tennis well. (用 good) → He is _______________

✅ 答案：good at tennis

29. She was angry with him because he was late. (用 for) → She was angry with him _______________

✅ 答案：for being late

30. The students were disappointed with their exam results. (用 about) → The students were disappointed _______________

✅ 答案：about their exam results
