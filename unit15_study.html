Unit 15: Question Tags（附加疑問句）

附加疑問句是放在陳述句末尾的短小問句，用於徵求同意或確認信息。

15.1 基本規則

陳述句附加問句規則
This cake is delicious,isn't it?肯定 + 否定
You are Jerry's sister,aren't you?肯定 + 否定
Marco isn't there,is he?否定 + 肯定
He hasn't called,has he?否定 + 肯定

15.2 助動詞/情態動詞的使用

附加問句使用與陳述句相同的助動詞/情態動詞。

  • Paul was here yesterday, wasn't he?
  • You couldn't come last night, could you?
  • Jenny has gone to Japan, hasn't she?
  • Daniel had bought the cake, hadn't he?

如果陳述句沒有助動詞，用 do/does/did：

  • You like swimming, don't you?
  • Your uncle lives in Sha Tin, doesn't he?
  • She received the book, didn't she?

15.3 特殊情況

情況例句
I am → aren't I?I am right, aren't I?
have 表動作 → do/does/didYou always have breakfast at home, don't you?
have 表擁有 → have/has 或 do/doesYour father has a car, hasn't he / doesn't he?
somebody/someone → theySomeone called, didn't they?
nobody/no one → they (肯定tag)Nobody phoned, did they?
something/anything → itSomething fell off the shelf, didn't it?
nothing → it (肯定tag)Nothing happened, did it?
everything → itEverything is fine, isn't it?
this/that → itThis is your phone, isn't it?
these/those → theyThose aren't your books, are they?
There + be → 用 thereThere was an accident, wasn't there?
Let's → shall we?Let's go, shall we?
Imperative → won't you?Open the door, won't you?

考核要點 ✅

  1. 肯定 + 否定 tag；否定 + 肯定 tag
  2. 用相同助動詞；無助動詞用 do/does/did
  3. I am → aren't I
  4. somebody/nobody → they；something/nothing → it
  5. this/that → it；these/those → they
  6. There + be → there
  7. Let's → shall we?
