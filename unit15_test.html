Unit 15: Question Tags — Practice Test

總分：100分 | 時限：40分鐘

Section A: MC 10題（每題3分）

1. This cake is delicious, ______?

✅ 答案：B

2. You like swimming, ______?

✅ 答案：A

3. He hasn't called back, ______?

✅ 答案：A

4. I am right, ______?

✅ 答案：C

5. Someone called, ______?

✅ 答案：A

6. Nothing happened, ______?

✅ 答案：A

7. This is your phone, ______?

✅ 答案：C

8. Let's go for a walk, ______?

✅ 答案：C

9. Your father has a car, ______?

✅ 答案：C

10. Nobody phoned for me, ______?

✅ 答案：A

Section B: Fill in 10題（每題3分）

11. This cake is delicious, _______________?

✅ 答案：isn't it

12. You like swimming, _______________?

✅ 答案：don't you

13. He hasn't called back, _______________?

✅ 答案：has he

14. I am right, _______________?

✅ 答案：aren't I

15. Someone called, _______________?

✅ 答案：didn't they

16. Nothing happened, _______________?

✅ 答案：did it

17. This is your phone, _______________?

✅ 答案：isn't it

18. Let's go, _______________?

✅ 答案：shall we

19. Paul was here yesterday, _______________?

✅ 答案：wasn't he

20. Everything is fine, _______________?

✅ 答案：isn't it

Section C: Error Correction 5題（每題4分）

21. This cake is delicious, is it?

✅ isn't it

22. I am right, am I not?

✅ aren't I

23. Someone called, didn't it?

✅ didn't they

24. Nothing happened, didn't it?

✅ did it

25. Let's go, will you?

✅ shall we

Section D: Rewriting 5題（每題4分）

26. Add a question tag: You are Jerry's sister → You are Jerry's sister, _______________

✅ 答案：aren't you

27. Add a question tag: Marco isn't there → Marco isn't there, _______________

✅ 答案：is he

28. Add a question tag: You can swim → You can swim, _______________

✅ 答案：can't you

29. Add a question tag: She had a good time → She had a good time, _______________

✅ 答案：didn't she

30. Add a question tag: There was an accident → There was an accident, _______________

✅ 答案：wasn't there
