Unit 16: Participle Phrases — Practice Test

總分：100分 | 時限：40分鐘

Section A: MC 10題（每題3分）

1. ______ the siren, the driver gave way to the ambulance.

✅ 答案：B

2. ______ by the fight, Sheldon left the room.

✅ 答案：B

3. ______ beside the river, Janice bumped into an old friend.

✅ 答案：B

4. ______ satisfied with his novel, the writer started again.

✅ 答案：A

5. The girl ______ on stage is Peter's sister.

✅ 答案：B

6. ______ on the bus, Chris missed his stop.

✅ 答案：B

7. ______ big Hollywood stars, the film had a high budget.

✅ 答案：B

8. ______ that her uncle had passed away, Annie cried.

✅ 答案：B

9. The woman ______ a long brown jacket is Simon's mother.

✅ 答案：B

10. Players ______ all questions correctly will win a prize.

✅ 答案：B

Section B: Fill in 10題（每題3分）

11. _______________ (Hear) the siren, the driver gave way.

✅ 答案：Hearing

12. _______________ (Annoy) by the fight, Sheldon left the room.

✅ 答案：Annoyed

13. _______________ (Jog) beside the river, Janice bumped into a friend.

✅ 答案：Jogging

14. _______________ satisfied with his novel, the writer started again.

✅ 答案：Not

15. The girl _______________ (sing) on stage is Peter's sister.

✅ 答案：singing

16. _______________ (fall) asleep on the bus, Chris missed his stop.

✅ 答案：Falling

17. The film, _______________ (feature) big stars, had a high budget.

✅ 答案：featuring

18. _______________ (learn) the news, Annie cried.

✅ 答案：Learning

19. The woman _______________ (wear) a brown jacket is Simon's mother.

✅ 答案：wearing

20. _______________ (give) a squeaky toy, the dog ran away happily.

✅ 答案：Given

Section C: Error Correction 5題（每題4分）

21. Hear the siren, the driver gave way.

✅ Hearing the siren, the driver gave way.

22. Annoying by the fight, Sheldon left the room.

✅ Annoyed by the fight, Sheldon left the room.

23. Not eat enough, the hiker got lost.

✅ Not having eaten enough, the hiker got lost.

24. The girl singing on stage is Peter's sister. (移除不必要的逗號)

✅ No error

25. Simon's mother worried about his safety called the police.

✅ Simon's mother, worried about his safety, called the police.

Section D: Rewriting 5題（每題4分）

26. The driver heard the siren. He gave way. (合併為分詞短語) → _______________

✅ 答案：Hearing the siren, the driver gave way.

27. Sheldon was annoyed by the fight. He left the room. (合併) → _______________

✅ 答案：Annoyed by the fight, Sheldon left the room.

28. Janice was jogging beside the river. She bumped into a friend. (合併) → _______________

✅ 答案：Jogging beside the river, Janice bumped into a friend.

29. Chris fell asleep on the bus. He missed his stop. (合併) → _______________

✅ 答案：Falling asleep on the bus, Chris missed his stop.

30. The writer was not satisfied with his novel. He decided to start again. (合併) → _______________

✅ 答案：Not satisfied with his novel, the writer decided to start again.
