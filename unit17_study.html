Unit 17: Relative Clauses（關係從句）

本單元複習並深入學習限定性（defining）和非限定性（non-defining）關係從句。

17.1 Defining Relative Clauses（限定性關係從句）

提供必要信息來辨識所談論的人或事物，不用逗號。

關係代詞用法例句
who / that指人（主語）The person who/that wrote this song is a friend of my sister's.
whom / who / that指人（受詞，可省略）The manager (whom/who/that) I talked to was very helpful.
which / that指物The tree which/that was blown down was very old.
when指時間It was windy on the day when I got to Hong Kong.
where指地點The park where we used to play has been renovated.
why指原因The reason why he called was to tell me the news.

17.2 省略關係代詞

當關係代詞在從句中作受詞時，可以省略。

  • The girl (whom/who/that) Kenneth has admired for years is Sally.
  • Do you know the brand of chocolate (which/that) Christy loves?
⚠️ 注意

關係代詞作主語時 不能省略：
✗ The picture on the wall contains a clue will help us. →
✓ The picture on the wall contains a clue which/that will help us.

17.3 不要用 what

關係從句中不要用 what 代替 which/that。

⚠️ 易錯點

✗ Have you watched the movie what I talked to you about?
✓ Have you watched the movie which/that I talked to you about?

17.4 Non-defining Relative Clauses（非限定性關係從句）

提供額外信息，用逗號分隔，信息可有可無。不能用 that。

  • Johnny Wong, whom we met yesterday, texted me just now.
  • Joey, who is currently on a working holiday, is coming home this summer.
  • Tyler forgot to bring his book, which is unlike him.
Defining（限定）Non-defining（非限定）
必要性必要信息額外信息
逗號不用逗號用逗號
that可用 that不能用 that
省略受詞可省略不可省略

考核要點 ✅

  1. who/which/that/when/where/why 引導限定性關係從句
  2. 受詞位置的關係代詞可省略；主語位置不可省略
  3. 不要用 what 替代 which/that
  4. 非限定性關係從句用逗號、不能用 that、不能省略關係代詞
