Unit 17: Relative Clauses — Practice Test

總分：100分 | 時限：40分鐘

Section A: MC 10題（每題3分）

1. The person ______ wrote this song is my friend.

✅ 答案：B

2. The tree ______ was blown down was very old.

✅ 答案：C

3. Have you watched the movie ______ I talked to you about?

✅ 答案：C

4. The manager ______ I talked to was very helpful.

✅ 答案：D

5. Johnny Wong, ______ we met yesterday, texted me.

✅ 答案：C

6. Non-defining relative clauses use ______.

✅ 答案：A

7. The park ______ we used to play has been renovated.

✅ 答案：B

8. The reason ______ he called was to tell me the news.

✅ 答案：C

9. In defining relative clauses, the object pronoun can be ______.

✅ 答案：A

10. ______ cannot be used in non-defining relative clauses.

✅ 答案：D

Section B: Fill in 10題（每題3分）

11. The person _______________ (who/which) wrote this song is my friend.

✅ 答案：who || that

12. The tree _______________ (who/which) was blown down was very old.

✅ 答案：which || that

13. The manager _______________ I talked to was very helpful. (可省略？填入答案)

✅ 答案：(whom/who/that) || (可以省略) || omit || omitted

14. Have you watched the movie _______________ I talked about? (不要用 what)

✅ 答案：which || that

15. Johnny Wong, _______________ we met yesterday, texted me.

✅ 答案：whom || who

16. The park _______________ we used to play has been renovated.

✅ 答案：where

17. The reason _______________ he called was to tell me the news.

✅ 答案：why

18. A defining relative clause gives _______________ information.

✅ 答案：necessary || essential

19. Non-defining relative clauses use _______________.

✅ 答案：commas

20. _______________ cannot be used in non-defining relative clauses.

✅ 答案：That

Section C: Error Correction 5題（每題4分）

21. Have you watched the movie what I talked to you about?

✅ Have you watched the movie which/that I talked to you about?

22. The picture contains a clue will help us solve the riddle.

✅ The picture contains a clue which/that will help us solve the riddle.

23. Johnny Wong, that we met yesterday, texted me.

✅ Johnny Wong, whom/who we met yesterday, texted me.

24. The park which we used to play has been renovated.

✅ The park where we used to play has been renovated.

25. The person wrote this song is my friend.

✅ The person who/that wrote this song is my friend.

Section D: Rewriting 5題（每題4分）

26. The person wrote this song. She is my friend. (合併) → The person _______________

✅ 答案：who/that wrote this song is my friend.

27. I talked to the manager. He was helpful. (合併) → The manager _______________

✅ 答案：(whom/who/that) I talked to was helpful.

28. The tree was blown down. It was very old. (合併) → The tree _______________

✅ 答案：which/that was blown down was very old.

29. We used to play in the park. It has been renovated. (用 where) → The park _______________

✅ 答案：where we used to play has been renovated.

30. He called me. The reason was to tell me the news. (用 why) → The reason _______________

✅ 答案：why he called was to tell me the news.
