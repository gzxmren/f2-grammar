Unit 18: Reported Speech (Orders) — Practice Test

總分：100分 | 時限：35分鐘

Section A: MC 10題（每題3分）

1. "Please sit down," the teacher said. → The teacher ______ us to sit down.

✅ 答案：B

2. "Don't be late," Mum said. → Mum asked me ______ late.

✅ 答案：C

3. "Get out of the car!" → The policeman ______ the driver to get out.

✅ 答案：C

4. "Please help me," she said. → She ______ me to help her.

✅ 答案：C

5. "Don't run in the corridor!" → The teacher told us ______ in the corridor.

✅ 答案：C

6. "Stay where you are!" → The officer ______ them to stay where they were.

✅ 答案：B

7. "Please wait here," the receptionist said. → The receptionist requested us ______ here.

✅ 答案：B

8. "Don't touch the paintings," the guide said. → The guide instructed us ______ the paintings.

✅ 答案：C

9. "Finish your homework first," Mum said. → Mum told me ______ my homework first.

✅ 答案：B

10. "Don't forget your keys," Dad said. → Dad reminded me ______ my keys.

✅ 答案：C

Section B: Fill in 10題（每題3分）

11. "Please sit down" → The teacher asked us _______________.

✅ 答案：to sit down

12. "Don't be late" → Mum told me _______________.

✅ 答案：not to be late

13. "Get out of the car!" → The policeman ordered the driver _______________.

✅ 答案：to get out of the car

14. "Please help me" → She asked him _______________.

✅ 答案：to help her

15. "Finish your homework" → Dad told me _______________.

✅ 答案：to finish my homework

16. "Don't touch the paintings" → The guide instructed us _______________.

✅ 答案：not to touch the paintings

17. "Wait here" → The receptionist asked us _______________.

✅ 答案：to wait here

18. "Don't run" → The teacher told the students _______________.

✅ 答案：not to run

19. "Stay where you are" → The officer commanded them _______________.

✅ 答案：to stay where they were

20. "Don't forget your keys" → Dad reminded me _______________.

✅ 答案：not to forget my keys

Section C: Error Correction 5題（每題4分）

21. She told me don't be late.

✅ She told me not to be late.

22. He asked to me wait.

✅ He asked me to wait.

23. The teacher said us to sit down.

✅ The teacher told us to sit down.

24. Mum asked don't run.

✅ Mum asked me not to run.

25. He ordered to them leave.

✅ He ordered them to leave.

Section D: Rewriting 5題（每題4分）

26. "Please wait here." (用ask) → She _______________

✅ 答案：asked me to wait here

27. "Don't be late." (用tell) → He _______________

✅ 答案：told me not to be late

28. "Finish your work." (用instruct) → The boss _______________

✅ 答案：instructed me to finish my work

29. "Don't run." (用tell) → The teacher _______________

✅ 答案：told us not to run

30. "Stay where you are." (用order) → The officer _______________

✅ 答案：ordered them to stay where they were
