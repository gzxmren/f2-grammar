Unit 19: Reported Speech (Offers) — Test

總分：100分 | 時限：35分鐘

Section A: MC 10題（每題3分）

1. Brian offered ______ Sally home.

✅ 答案：B

2. The doctor advised Melinda ______ plenty of water.

✅ 答案：B

3. Lily suggested ______ for a swim.

✅ 答案：C

4. He refused ______ for the damage.

✅ 答案：B

5. She promised ______ early.

✅ 答案：B

6. Dad cautioned me ______ out alone at night.

✅ 答案：C

7. He agreed ______ me.

✅ 答案：B

8. She reminded me ______ my keys.

✅ 答案：C

9. He recommended ______ the early train.

✅ 答案：C

10. She suggested that we ______ early.

✅ 答案：A

Section B: Fill in 10題（每題3分）

11. Brian offered _______________ (take) Sally home.

✅ 答案：to take

12. The doctor advised me _______________ (drink) more water.

✅ 答案：to drink

13. Lily suggested _______________ (go) for a swim.

✅ 答案：going

14. He refused _______________ (pay).

✅ 答案：to pay

15. She promised _______________ (come) early.

✅ 答案：to come

16. Dad cautioned me _______________ (not/go) out alone.

✅ 答案：not to go

17. He agreed _______________ (help) me.

✅ 答案：to help

18. She recommended _______________ (take) the early train.

✅ 答案：taking

19. He suggested that we _______________ (go) early.

✅ 答案：go || should go

20. I advised him _______________ (see) a doctor.

✅ 答案：to see

Section C: Error Correction 5題（每題4分）

21. Brian offered taking Sally home.

✅ Brian offered to take Sally home.

22. He suggested me to go swimming.

✅ He suggested going swimming. / He suggested that we go swimming.

23. She promised coming early.

✅ She promised to come early.

24. Dad cautioned me don't go out alone.

✅ Dad cautioned me not to go out alone.

25. He refused paying for the damage.

✅ He refused to pay for the damage.

Section D: Rewriting 5題（每題4分）

26. "I will help you" (用offer) → He _______________

✅ 答案：offered to help me

27. "You should rest" (用advise) → The doctor _______________

✅ 答案：advised me to rest

28. "Let's go swimming" (用suggest) → She _______________

✅ 答案：suggested going swimming || suggested that we go swimming

29. "I won't pay" (用refuse) → He _______________

✅ 答案：refused to pay

30. "I'll be on time" (用promise) → She _______________

✅ 答案：promised to be on time
