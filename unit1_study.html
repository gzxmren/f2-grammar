Unit 1: Talking about the present

本教材涵蓋 Simple Present Tense（一般現在式）、Present Continuous Tense（現在進行式）、Stative Verbs（狀態動詞） 以及 Verb 'have' 的特殊用法。適合香港中二學生應付校內考試及日常語法運用。

1.1 Simple Present Tense（一般現在式）

使用時機

使用場景說明例句
永恆事實 / 真理
(Facts/general truths)		不受時間影響、任何時候都成立的事Light travels faster than sound.
The sun rises in the east.
Water boils at 100°C.
此刻真實的情況
(Present states)		說話當下仍然成立的狀態或情況My father works for a software company.
She lives in Kowloon.
I have two brothers.
習慣 / 定期做的事
(Habits/routines)		重複發生的動作或日常生活習慣I usually wake up at 7 a.m.
They play basketball every Sunday.
He takes the MTR to school.

常見時間副詞（Adverbs of Frequency）

這些副詞通常放在 主語之後、動詞之前（但 am/is/are 之後）：

副詞頻率例句
always100%（總是）He always arrives on time.
usually90%（通常）I usually have toast for breakfast.
often70%（經常）She often reads before bed.
sometimes50%（有時）We sometimes eat out on weekends.
rarely10%（很少）They rarely watch TV.
never0%（從不）Tom never eats spicy food.

其他常見時間表達（通常放在句末）：

  • every day / every week / every month
  • once a week / twice a month / three times a year
  • from time to time（偶爾）
  • on Mondays / at weekends
💡 提示

sometimes 可以放在句首、句中或句尾，位置較靈活。

Sometimes I go jogging. / I sometimes go jogging. / I go jogging sometimes.

動詞變化規則（Third-person Singular -s / -es）

主語為 he / she / it / 單數名詞 時，動詞要加 -s 或 -es：

規則例子
大部分動詞直接加 -swork → works, live → lives, eat → eats
以 -ch, -sh, -s, -x, -o 結尾 → 加 -eswatch → watches, finish → finishes, go → goes, do → does
以 子音 + y 結尾 → 改 y 為 i 再加 -esstudy → studies, cry → cries, fly → flies
以 母音 + y 結尾 → 直接加 -splay → plays, say → says, enjoy → enjoys
have 改為 hasI have → He has
⚠️ 注意

助動詞 do / does 用於否定句和疑問句：

She does not (doesn't) like coffee. / Does she like coffee?

1.2 Present Continuous Tense（現在進行式）

結構：am / is / are + 現在分詞（V-ing）

使用時機

使用場景說明例句
說話時正在發生的事
(Actions happening now)		就在此刻正在進行的動作The doorbell is ringing.
Please be quiet — I am studying.
Look! It is raining.
目前階段發生的事（臨時事件）
(Temporary situations around now)		近期一段時間正在進行，但不一定說話當下正在做I am writing an article about white dolphins.
She is staying with her aunt this month.
They are learning Chinese this year.

常見時間副詞

  • now — She is doing her homework now.
  • right now — He is taking a shower right now.
  • at the moment — I'm busy at the moment.
  • presently — The manager is presently in a meeting.
  • still — It is still raining.
  • these days / this week / this month — We are preparing for the exam this week.

1.2 與 Simple Present 的對比

比較點Simple PresentPresent Continuous
核心用法習慣、事實、常態正在進行、臨時情況
時間感永久或經常此刻或目前階段
標誌詞always, usually, every day, nevernow, at the moment, still, today
例句I live in Hong Kong.（長期居住）I am living with my cousin this summer.（短期暫住）
例句He works as a teacher.（職業）He is working on a project this month.（當下任務）
例句I have a camera, but I don't use it often.（擁有 + 習慣）I am using the camera right now.（此刻動作）

Stative Verbs（狀態動詞）

有些動詞表示 狀態而非動作，不能使用進行式（即使發生在「現在」）。

常見 Stative Verbs 分類

類別動詞
喜好 / 感受like, love, hate, dislike, prefer
意願 / 欲望want, wish, desire
認知 / 看法know, believe, understand, think（= believe）, agree, doubt, realise, remember, forget, mean
所屬關係belong, own, possess, have（= 擁有）
感官see, hear, smell, taste（= 品嘗起來）, feel（= 感覺）, seem, appear, look（= 看起來）
其他狀態be, cost, need, depend, matter, contain, include

正確 vs 錯誤 對比

✗ 錯誤（進行式）✓ 正確（簡單式）
I am wanting a pet cat.I want a pet cat.
She is knowing the answer.She knows the answer.
He is liking this song.He likes this song.
We are understanding the rule.We understand the rule.
This is belonging to me.This belongs to me.
I am seeing the teacher now.（見到）I see the teacher now.
💡 進階提示

部分 stative verbs 在轉換意義後可以用進行式：

I think you are right.（認為 — 狀態，不能用進行式）

I am thinking about the problem.（思考 — 動作，可以用進行式）

The soup tastes good.（嘗起來 — 狀態）

The chef is tasting the soup.（品嘗 — 動作）
