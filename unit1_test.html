Unit 1: Talking about the Present — Practice Test

總分：30 題 | 建議時限：35 分鐘

本測驗涵蓋 Simple Present Tense、Present Continuous Tense、Stative Verbs 及 Verb 'have' 的特殊用法。

Section A: Multiple Choice（選擇題）— 10 題

1. Every morning, my mother ______ breakfast for the family.

✅ 答案：B — Simple Present 第三人稱單數（mother = she），表示每日習慣。

2. Look! The children ______ football in the playground.

✅ 答案：C — 「Look!」提示正在發生，用 Present Continuous；children 複數。

3. I ______ what you mean. Could you explain again?

✅ 答案：A — understand 是 stative verb，不能用進行式。

4. This dress ______ soft and comfortable.

✅ 答案：B — feel 作「摸起來」是 stative verb，不用進行式。

5. My brother ______ a shower right now, so please call back later.

✅ 答案：C — have 在此解「洗澡」（動作），可用進行式。

6. Water ______ at 100 degrees Celsius.

✅ 答案：C — 科學真理用 Simple Present。

7. We usually ______ to the cinema on Saturdays, but this week we ______ at home.

✅ 答案：B — usually 提示習慣；this week 提示臨時情況。

8. The chef ______ the soup in the kitchen at the moment.

✅ 答案：B — taste 在此解「品嘗」（動作），可用進行式。

9. I ______ a headache. Can I take a rest?

✅ 答案：B — headache 是狀態，have 不用進行式。

10. She ______ to learn Japanese this year because she wants to visit Tokyo.

✅ 答案：B — this year 提示目前階段的臨時計劃，用 Present Continuous。

Section B: Fill in the Blanks（填空）— 10 題

Fill in the blanks with the correct form of the verb. Use Simple Present or Present Continuous.

11. The shop assistant usually _______________ (stand) behind the counter, but today she _______________ (help) customers on the floor.

✅ 答案：stands; is helping

12. Please be quiet! I _______________ (try) to concentrate on my homework.

✅ 答案：am trying / 'm trying

13. My grandparents _______________ (live) in a small village in the New Territories. They _______________ (grow) their own vegetables.

✅ 答案：live; grow

14. I _______________ (not / believe) his story. It sounds too strange.

✅ 答案：don't believe

15. Hurry up! Everyone _______________ (wait) for you outside.

✅ 答案：is waiting

16. He _______________ (have) a fever, so he _______________ (stay) in bed today.

✅ 答案：has; is staying

17. The train to Kowloon _______________ (leave) at 8:30 every evening.

✅ 答案：leaves

18. We _______________ (usually / spend) our summer holiday in Japan, but this year we _______________ (go) to Korea instead.

✅ 答案：usually spend; are going

19. I _______________ (want) a new phone, but I _______________ (not / have) enough money.

✅ 答案：want; don't have

20. Listen! Someone _______________ (knock) on the door. I _______________ (think) it's the postman.

✅ 答案：is knocking; think

Section C: Error Correction（改錯）— 5 題

Each sentence has ONE grammar mistake. Write the corrected sentence.

21. I am wanting to buy a birthday present for my best friend.

✅ I want to buy a birthday present for my best friend.

22. She is having a car, but she doesn't drive it every day.

✅ She has a car, but she doesn't drive it every day.

23. Look at that man! He wears a funny hat today.

✅ Look at that man! He is wearing a funny hat today.

24. The students are understanding the lesson because the teacher explains very clearly.

✅ The students understand the lesson because the teacher explains very clearly.

25. I am going to the gym every evening after school.

✅ I go to the gym every evening after school.

Section D: Sentence Rewriting（句子改寫）— 5 題

Rewrite the sentences according to the prompts.

26. Peter plays the piano very well. (改否定)

✅ Peter does not (doesn't) play the piano very well.

27. The children are doing their homework in the library. (改疑問句)

✅ Are the children doing their homework in the library?

28. I eat fast food. (用 rarely 改寫)

✅ I rarely eat fast food.

29. She usually wears glasses, but today she is wearing contact lenses. (對比習慣vs今天)

✅ She usually wears glasses, but today she is wearing contact lenses.

30. I have lunch with my friends every Saturday. (This Saturday is different, I'm busy. 用Present Continuous)

✅ I am not having lunch with my friends this Saturday because I am busy.

得分：—

正確 / 總數：0 / 0
