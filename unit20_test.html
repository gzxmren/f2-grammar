Unit 20: Adverbs — Practice Test

總分：100分 | 時限：35分鐘

Section A: MC 10題（每題3分）

1. She always does her homework ______.

✅ 答案：B

2. He broke his leg ______ he was playing badminton.

✅ 答案：B

3. Don't behave ______ a child.

✅ 答案：B

4. She is leaving ______ 24 August.

✅ 答案：B

5. The local people are very ______ to visitors.

✅ 答案：A

6. He speaks English ______.

✅ 答案：B

7. The phone rang ______ I was leaving.

✅ 答案：A

8. Please draw it ______ I described it.

✅ 答案：B

9. She'll leave ______ she gets her visa.

✅ 答案：A

10. He went home quickly ______ school.

✅ 答案：B

Section B: Fill in 10題（每題3分）

11. She always does her homework _______________ (careful → 副詞).

✅ 答案：carefully

12. He speaks English _______________ (fluent → 副詞).

✅ 答案：fluently

13. The children are playing _______________ (happy) in the park.

✅ 答案：happily

14. He broke his leg _______________ (當) playing badminton.

✅ 答案：while

15. Don't behave _______________ (像) a child.

✅ 答案：like

16. Please draw it _______________ (按照) I described it.

✅ 答案：as

17. She'll leave _______________ (一⋯就) she gets her visa.

✅ 答案：as soon as

18. The phone rang _______________ (正當) I was leaving.

✅ 答案：just as

19. He went _______________ (回家) quickly after school.

✅ 答案：home

20. I slept very _______________ (好) last night.

✅ 答案：well

Section C: Error Correction 5題（每題4分）

21. She speaks English fluent.

✅ She speaks English fluently.

22. Don't behave as a child.

✅ Don't behave like a child.

23. He broke his leg during he was playing.

✅ while he was playing

24. The local people are friendlily.

✅ friendly (adj)

25. She is leaving in 24 August.

✅ on 24 August

Section D: Rewriting 5題（每題4分）

26. He is a careful driver. He drives _______________

✅ 答案：carefully

27. She is a fluent speaker. She speaks _______________

✅ 答案：fluently

28. I left. Then she called me. (用 when) → She called _______________

✅ 答案：when I left

29. He behaves like a child. Don't _______________

✅ 答案：behave like a child

30. Please draw it according to my description. (用 as) → Draw it _______________

✅ 答案：as I described it
