Unit 21: Modal Verbs — Advice & Possibility（情態動詞：建議與可能性）

21.1 給予建議

情態動詞用法例句
should一般建議You should drink more water.
ought to與 should 相似You ought to see a doctor.
had better (not)強烈/緊急建議You'd better stop texting. You'd better not forget.
must非常強烈的建議You must take this course.
can/could提出可能解決方案You can borrow mine.

21.2 其他建議表達

表達方式例句
It's a good idea to...It's a good idea to attend more practice sessions.
It is advised/recommended that...It is recommended that you (should) pack warm clothes.
Let's...Let's go to the beach.
Why not / Why don't you...Why not save up for a new laptop?
Shall we...Shall we postpone the presentation?
How about / What about...How about naming your dog Fluffy?
If I were you...If I were you, I would take the MTR.

21.3 可能性（Present/Future）

情態動詞可能性例句
may/might也許（低-中）I may/might finish it by tomorrow.
could可能（較不確定）There could be one in my drawer.
should應該（相當確定）She should be here by now.
must一定是（非常確定）He must be at home.
can't不可能是（否定）That can't be true.

21.4 可能性（Past）

結構含義例句
may/might have + V-ed₂過去也許The AC may have stopped working.
could have + V-ed₂過去可能She could have taken the wrong bus.
should have + V-ed₂本該You should have received it yesterday.
must have + V-ed₂過去一定He must have forgotten.
can't have + V-ed₂過去不可能She can't have left already.

考核要點 ✅

  1. should/ought to/had better/must 強度遞增
  2. had better (not) + 原形動詞（緊急建議）
  3. suggest/recommend + that 從句或 -ing
  4. If I were you... (Type 2 條件句)
  5. should = 預期；must = 肯定；can't = 不可能
  6. may have / could have / must have + V-ed₂（過去推測）
