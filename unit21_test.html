Unit 21: Modal Verbs — Practice Test

總分：100分 | 時限：40分鐘

Section A: MC 10題（每題3分）

1. You look pale. You ______ see a doctor.

✅ 答案：A

2. I've bought the tickets. You ______ forget about our date.

✅ 答案：A

3. Where is Joey? She ______ be here by now.

✅ 答案：B

4. The AC ______ have stopped working. It's very hot.

✅ 答案：C

5. He must ______ forgotten our meeting.

✅ 答案：A

6. You should ______ your exam results yesterday.

✅ 答案：C

7. If I ______ you, I would take the MTR.

✅ 答案：C

8. She can't ______ left already. It's only 4 p.m.

✅ 答案：A

9. ______ we postpone the presentation?

✅ 答案：C

10. You ______ borrow my book if you need it.

✅ 答案：B

Section B: Fill in 10題（每題3分）

11. You _______________ (最好) see a doctor.

✅ 答案：had better || 'd better

12. You _______________ (最好別) forget the tickets.

✅ 答案：had better not || 'd better not

13. She _______________ (應該) be here by now.

✅ 答案：should

14. The AC _______________ (可能) have stopped working.

✅ 答案：may || might

15. He _______________ (一定) have forgotten.

✅ 答案：must

16. You should _______________ (have/receive) the results yesterday.

✅ 答案：have received

17. If I _______________ (be) you, I would go.

✅ 答案：were

18. She _______________ (不可能) have left already.

✅ 答案：can't

19. _______________ (咱們) go to the beach.

✅ 答案：Let's

20. How _______________ (⋯⋯怎麼樣) going for a walk?

✅ 答案：about

Section C: Error Correction 5題（每題4分）

21. You had better to see a doctor.

✅ You had better see a doctor.

22. You hadn't better forget.

✅ You had better not forget.

23. He must forgotten our meeting.

✅ He must have forgotten our meeting.

24. If I am you, I would go.

✅ If I were you, I would go.

25. She can't has left already.

✅ She can't have left already.

Section D: Rewriting 5題（每題4分）

26. It would be a good idea to see a doctor. (用 should) → You _______________

✅ 答案：should see a doctor

27. I advise you not to forget the tickets. (用 had better) → You _______________

✅ 答案：had better not forget the tickets

28. Perhaps she has taken the wrong bus. (用 may) → She _______________

✅ 答案：may have taken the wrong bus

29. It is impossible that he has left. (用 can't) → He _______________

✅ 答案：can't have left

30. I think it's certain that he forgot. (用 must) → He _______________

✅ 答案：must have forgotten
