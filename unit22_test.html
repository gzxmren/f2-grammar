Unit 22: Preferences — Practice Test

總分：100分 | 時限：35分鐘

Section A: MC 10題（每題3分）

1. I'd like ________ a song.

✅ 答案：B

2. I'd rather ________ ramen than sushi.

✅ 答案：A

3. I'd rather not ________ a horror film.

✅ 答案：A

4. I prefer Japanese food ________ Thai food.

✅ 答案：A

5. I'd prefer ________ a nap rather than go out.

✅ 答案：B

6. I'd love ________ to the concert with you.

✅ 答案：B

7. I like dancing more ________ singing.

✅ 答案：B

8. Of all fruits, I like apples ________.

✅ 答案：D

9. Would you like ________ to the party?

✅ 答案：B

10. Janice would prefer not ________ to Ocean Park.

✅ 答案：B

Section B: Fill in 10題（每題3分）

11. I'd like _______________ (sing) a song.

✅ 答案：to sing

12. I'd rather _______________ (have) tea than coffee.

✅ 答案：have

13. I'd rather not _______________ (watch) a horror film.

✅ 答案：watch

14. I prefer Japanese food _______________ Thai food.

✅ 答案：to

15. I'd prefer _______________ (take) a nap.

✅ 答案：to take

16. I'd love _______________ (go) to the concert.

✅ 答案：to go

17. I like dancing more _______________ singing.

✅ 答案：than

18. Of all fruits, I like apples _______________.

✅ 答案：the most || most

19. Would you like _______________ (come) to the party?

✅ 答案：to come

20. I'd rather have tea _______________ coffee.

✅ 答案：than

Section C: Error Correction 5題（每題4分）

21. I'd like sing a song.

✅ I'd like to sing a song.

22. I'd rather to have tea than coffee.

✅ I'd rather have tea than coffee.

23. I prefer Japanese food than Thai food.

✅ I prefer Japanese food to Thai food.

24. I'd love go to the concert.

✅ I'd love to go to the concert.

25. I'd rather not to watch that film.

✅ I'd rather not watch that film.

Section D: Rewriting 5題（每題4分）

26. I want to sing. (用 would like) → I'd _______________

✅ 答案：like to sing

27. I prefer tea to coffee. (用 rather) → I'd rather _______________

✅ 答案：have tea than coffee

28. I don't want to watch a horror film. (用 rather) → I'd _______________

✅ 答案：rather not watch a horror film

29. I prefer Japanese food. (用 more than) → I like Japanese food _______________

✅ 答案：more than Thai food

30. I want very much to go to the concert. (用 love) → I'd _______________

✅ 答案：love to go to the concert
