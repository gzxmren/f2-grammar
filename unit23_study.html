Unit 23: Connectives（連接詞）

使用連接詞將觀點聯繫起來，表達附加、對比、原因和結果。

23.1 附加（Addition）

連接詞例句
andHe argued with her and she doesn't talk to him.
alsoAmy's job is mentally demanding. Also, I was very tired.
besides / moreover / furthermoreThis app is not user-friendly. Besides/Moreover, it's expensive.
in additionIn addition, there is a pattern on the back.

23.2 對比（Contrast）

連接詞例句
butHe spoke very fast, but I had no difficulty.
orWhich subject will you choose, physics or economics?

23.3 原因（Reason）

連接詞例句
because / as / sinceWe stayed home because/as/since it was raining.
because of / due to (+ 名詞)We stayed home because of the rain.

23.4 結果（Result）

連接詞例句
soIt has been raining for weeks, so the reservoir overflowed.
(and) as a resultJoyce's foot was injured, and as a result she couldn't dance.
therefore / thus / consequentlyTherefore/Thus/Consequently, she could not take part.

23.5 目的（Purpose）

連接詞例句
in order to / so as toHe leaves early in order to avoid traffic.
so that (+ 從句)He took a taxi so that he wouldn't be late.

23.6 舉例（Examples）

  • for example / for instance: Many sports, for example swimming and cycling, keep you fit.
  • such as / like: Bright colours, such as red and orange, were required.

考核要點 ✅

  1. 附加：and, also, besides, moreover, furthermore, in addition
  2. 原因：because/as/since + 從句；because of/due to + 名詞
  3. 結果：so, as a result, therefore, thus, consequently
  4. 目的：in order to, so as to (+ 動詞)；so that (+ 從句)
  5. 舉例：for example, for instance, such as, like
