Unit 23: Connectives — Practice Test

總分：100分 | 時限：40分鐘

Section A: MC 10題（每題3分）

1. He spoke very fast, ______ I had no difficulty.

✅ 答案：B

2. We stayed home ______ the rain.

✅ 答案：B

3. It has been raining for weeks, ______ the reservoir overflowed.

✅ 答案：C

4. He leaves early ______ avoid traffic.

✅ 答案：B

5. Joyce's foot was injured, and ______ she couldn't dance.

✅ 答案：A

6. He took a taxi ______ he wouldn't be late.

✅ 答案：C

7. Which subject will you choose, physics ______ economics?

✅ 答案：B

8. This app is not user-friendly. ______, it's expensive.

✅ 答案：C

9. Bright colours, ______ red and orange, were required.

✅ 答案：A

10. ______, she could not take part in the competition.

✅ 答案：B

Section B: Fill in 10題（每題3分）

11. He spoke very fast, _______________ (但是) I understood.

✅ 答案：but

12. We stayed home _______________ (因為) the rain.

✅ 答案：because of || due to

13. It rained, _______________ (所以) we stayed home.

✅ 答案：so

14. He leaves early _______________ (為了) avoid traffic.

✅ 答案：in order to || so as to

15. He took a taxi _______________ (以便) he wouldn't be late.

✅ 答案：so that

16. This app is not user-friendly. _______________ (此外), it's expensive.

✅ 答案：Moreover || Furthermore || Besides || In addition

17. Joyce's foot was injured, and _______________ (結果) she couldn't dance.

✅ 答案：as a result || therefore || consequently

18. Bright colours, _______________ (例如) red and orange, were required.

✅ 答案：such as || like || for example || for instance

19. We stayed home _______________ (因為) it was raining.

✅ 答案：because || as || since

20. _______________ (因此), she could not take part.

✅ 答案：Therefore || Thus || Consequently || As a result

Section C: Error Correction 5題（每題4分）

21. He spoke very fast, so I had no difficulty.

✅ but I had no difficulty.

22. We stayed home because of it was raining.

✅ because it was raining / because of the rain

23. He took a taxi for not being late.

✅ so that he wouldn't be late / in order not to be late

24. He leaves early so that avoid traffic.

✅ in order to avoid traffic / so that he can avoid traffic

25. This app is not user-friendly. But it's expensive.

✅ Moreover/Furthermore/Besides, it's expensive.

Section D: Rewriting 5題（每題4分）

26. He worked hard. He passed the exam. (用 so) → He worked hard, _______________

✅ 答案：so he passed the exam

27. He was sick. He stayed home. (用 because) → He stayed home _______________

✅ 答案：because he was sick

28. He left early. He wanted to avoid traffic. (用 in order to) → He left early _______________

✅ 答案：in order to avoid traffic

29. The app is expensive. Also, it's not user-friendly. (用 moreover) → The app is expensive. _______________

✅ 答案：Moreover, it's not user-friendly

30. She was injured. She couldn't dance. (用 therefore) → She was injured. _______________

✅ 答案：Therefore, she couldn't dance
