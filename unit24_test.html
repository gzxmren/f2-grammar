Unit 24: More Connectives — Practice Test

總分：100分 | 時限：35分鐘

Section A: MC 10題（每題3分）

1. He spoke ______ fast that I couldn't understand.

✅ 答案：A

2. It was ______ a hot day that we stayed home.

✅ 答案：B

3. I was ______ tired that I fell asleep.

✅ 答案：A

4. He left early ______ he could catch the bus.

✅ 答案：A

5. She has ______ beautiful flowers!

✅ 答案：B

6. His application was rejected. ______ he couldn't get the job.

✅ 答案：C

7. You finish your work. ______, I'll start dinner.

✅ 答案：C

8. It was ______ good weather that we went to the beach.

✅ 答案：B

9. She was ______ a fool that she believed him.

✅ 答案：B

10. He was so foolish ______ he believed the story.

✅ 答案：B

Section B: Fill in 10題（每題3分）

11. He spoke _______________ fast that I couldn't understand him.

✅ 答案：so

12. It was _______________ a hot day that we stayed home.

✅ 答案：such

13. I was _______________ tired that I fell asleep.

✅ 答案：so

14. He left early _______________ (為了) he could catch the bus.

✅ 答案：so that

15. She has _______________ beautiful flowers!

✅ 答案：such

16. It was _______________ good weather that we went to the beach.

✅ 答案：such

17. His application was rejected. _______________ (結果), he couldn't get the job.

✅ 答案：As a consequence || Consequently || Therefore

18. You finish your work. _______________ (與此同時), I'll make dinner.

✅ 答案：Meanwhile || In the meantime

19. He was _______________ a fool that he believed the story.

✅ 答案：such

20. He was _______________ foolish that he believed the story.

✅ 答案：so

Section C: Proofreading 5題（每題4分）

21. He spoke such fast that I couldn't understand.

✅ He spoke so fast that I couldn't understand.

22. It was so hot day that we stayed home.

✅ It was such a hot day that we stayed home.

23. He left early so that he could catch the bus.

✅ No error

24. I was so tired, I fell asleep. (可以省略 that)

✅ No error (that can be omitted)

25. She has so beautiful flowers!

✅ She has such beautiful flowers!

Section D: Rewriting 5題（每題4分）

26. The weather was very hot. We stayed home. (用 so...that) → The weather was _______________

✅ 答案：so hot that we stayed home

27. We had a very hot day. We stayed home. (用 such...that) → It was _______________

✅ 答案：such a hot day that we stayed home

28. I was very tired. I fell asleep. (用 so...that) → I was _______________

✅ 答案：so tired that I fell asleep

29. He wants to catch the bus. He left early. (用 so that) → He left early _______________

✅ 答案：so that he could catch the bus

30. He didn't meet the requirements. He was rejected. (用 consequently) → He didn't meet the requirements. _______________

✅ 答案：Consequently, he was rejected
