Unit 25: Phrasal Verbs（短語動詞）

短語動詞由動詞 + 介詞/副詞構成，意思常與原動詞不同。

25.1 常見短語動詞

短語動詞意思例句
look after照顧I'm looking after my aunt.
look into調查The police are looking into the case.
put out撲滅They put out the fire in 40 minutes.
find out找出/發現He found out who the murderer was.
turn down拒絕She turned down the offer.
check in辦理入住You can't check in now.
get up起床She gets up at 8 o'clock.
care for喜歡He doesn't care for fishing.

25.2 可分隔 vs 不可分隔

類型例句
可分隔（代詞在中間）Put it out. / Turn it down.
不可分隔Look after her. / Look into the case.

25.3 正式 vs 非正式

  • 非正式（短語動詞）：look into the case / find out the truth
  • 正式（單字動詞）：investigate the case / discover the truth

考核要點 ✅

  1. look after = take care of；look into = investigate；put out = extinguish
  2. find out = discover；turn down = refuse；check in = register
  3. 代詞短語動詞：代詞放在動詞和介詞之間（put it out）
  4. 不可分短語動詞：受詞放在介詞後（look after her）
  5. 短語動詞用於非正式場合
