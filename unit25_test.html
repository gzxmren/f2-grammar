Unit 25: Phrasal Verbs — Practice Test

總分：100分 | 時限：35分鐘

Section A: MC 10題（每題3分）

1. Can you ______ my cat while I'm away?

✅ 答案：B

2. The firemen ______ the fire quickly.

✅ 答案：B

3. I need to ______ who broke the window.

✅ 答案：A

4. She ______ the job offer because it paid too little.

✅ 答案：B

5. The police are ______ the robbery case.

✅ 答案：B

6. You can't ______ now. The flight is leaving.

✅ 答案：B

7. He doesn't ______ fishing.

✅ 答案：A

8. She ______ at 6 a.m. every day.

✅ 答案：B

9. Please ______ the light when you leave.

✅ 答案：B

10. I can't ______ the meaning of this word.

✅ 答案：C

Section B: Fill in 10題（每題3分）

11. Can you _______________ (照顧) my cat?

✅ 答案：look after

12. The firemen _______________ (撲滅) the fire.

✅ 答案：put out

13. I need to _______________ (找出) who did this.

✅ 答案：find out

14. She _______________ (拒絕) the offer.

✅ 答案：turned down

15. The police are _______________ (調查) the case.

✅ 答案：looking into

16. You can't _______________ (辦理入住) now.

✅ 答案：check in

17. She _______________ (起床) at 6 a.m.

✅ 答案：gets up

18. He doesn't _______________ (喜歡) fishing.

✅ 答案：care for

19. Please _______________ (關掉) the lights.

✅ 答案：turn off || switch off

20. Look _______________ (查詢) the word in a dictionary.

✅ 答案：up

Section C: Error Correction 5題（每題4分）

21. Can you look my cat after?

✅ Can you look after my cat?

22. She turned down it.

✅ She turned it down.

23. The police are looking the case into.

✅ The police are looking into the case.

24. I need to find who did this out.

✅ I need to find out who did this.

25. Please put the fire out. (代詞改寫: Put _______)

✅ Put it out.

Section D: Rewriting 5題（每題4分）

26. Take care of my cat. (用短語動詞) → _______________

✅ 答案：Look after my cat

27. Investigate the case. (用短語動詞) → _______________

✅ 答案：Look into the case

28. I refuse the offer. (用短語動詞) → I _______________

✅ 答案：turn down the offer || turn it down

29. Discover who did this. (用短語動詞) → _______________

✅ 答案：Find out who did this

30. Extinguish the fire. (用短語動詞) → _______________

✅ 答案：Put out the fire || Put it out
