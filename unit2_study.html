Unit 2: Talking about the past 複習教材

本教材涵蓋 Simple Past Tense（一般過去式）、Past Continuous Tense（過去進行式）、Present Perfect Tense（現在完成式）、Present Perfect Continuous Tense（現在完成進行式）、Past Perfect Tense（過去完成式） 以及 Past Perfect Continuous Tense（過去完成進行式）。

2.1 Simple Past Tense（一般過去式）

使用場景說明例句
過去完成的動作過去某明確時間點發生並結束I visited my grandmother yesterday.
She bought a new dress last week.
過去的事實或狀態過去存在的事實或情況He lived in London when he was a child.
過去重複的習慣過去經常做的事My grandfather walked to school every day.

動詞變化規則

規則例子
大部分加 -edwork→worked, play→played
以 -e 結尾加 -dlive→lived, like→liked
子音+y 改y為i加-edstudy→studied, cry→cried
母音+y 直接加-edstay→stayed, play→played
重讀閉音節雙寫加-edstop→stopped, plan→planned
不規則動詞（需背誦）go→went, eat→ate, see→saw

常見時間副詞

  • yesterday — I met her yesterday.
  • last night / last week / last month / last year
  • ago — She left ten minutes ago.
  • in + 年份 — He was born in 2010.

2.2 Past Continuous Tense（過去進行式）

結構：was / were + 現在分詞（V-ing）

使用場景說明例句
過去某刻正在進行強調在過去某一刻正在發生I was doing homework at 8 p.m. last night.
兩個動作同時進行同時發生的兩個長動作While I was cooking, my brother was watching TV.
一個動作被另一個打斷長動作（進行式）被短動作（簡單式）打斷I was taking a shower when the phone rang.
營造背景氛圍描述故事背景The sun was shining, the birds were singing.

⚠️ Simple Past vs Past Continuous

Simple PastPast Continuous
動作已完成動作未完成（正在進行）
I watched a movie.（看完了）I was watching a movie.（還沒看完）

2.3 Present Perfect Tense（現在完成式）

結構：has / have + 過去分詞（past participle）

使用場景說明例句
經驗強調是否發生過，不關心具體時間I have visited Japan twice.
過去對現在有影響動作結果影響到現在I have lost my keys.（現在找不到）
持續到現在與 since / for 搭配We have lived here for ten years.

常見時間副詞

副詞意思例句
ever曾經（疑問句）Have you ever seen a ghost?
never從未I have never been to the US.
already已經She has already left.
yet尚未I haven't finished yet.
just剛剛They have just arrived.
since自從He has worked here since 2020.
for持續了We have known each other for five years.

2.4 Present Perfect Continuous（現在完成進行式）

結構：has / have + been + V-ing

使用場景說明例句
持續到現在的動作強調持續性和未完成I have been studying for three hours.
強調持續長度關注動作本身持續多久She has been waiting since 8 a.m.
對現在有可見結果結果是動作持續造成的You look tired. Have you been working all night?

Present Perfect vs Present Perfect Continuous

Present PerfectPresent Perfect Continuous
強調結果或完成強調持續的過程
I have read the book.（看完了）I have been reading the book.（還在看）
可用 stative verbs只用 action verbs
I have known him for years. ✅✗ I have been knowing him.

2.5 Past Perfect Tense（過去完成式）

結構：had + 過去分詞

使用場景說明例句
「過去的過去」先發生的動作用 Past PerfectWhen I arrived, the train had already left.
過去某時間前已完成截至過去某時間已完成By the time he was 18, he had visited 15 countries.

2.6 Past Perfect Continuous（過去完成進行式）

結構：had + been + V-ing

使用場景說明例句
過去某時間之前一直在進行強調持續When I got home, mom had been cooking for 2 hours.
過去動作的結果過去有明顯證據Her eyes were red because she had been crying.

2.7 六大時態對比總結表

時態結構核心用法常見時間詞
Simple PastV-ed / 不規則過去完成的動作yesterday, ago, last week
Past Continuouswas/were + V-ing過去某刻正在進行at that time, when, while
Present Perfecthave/has + V-ed₂過去到現在的經驗/結果ever, never, since, for
Present Perfect Continuoushave/has + been + V-ing持續到現在（強調過程）for, since, all day
Past Perfecthad + V-ed₂過去的過去by the time, before, after
Past Perfect Continuoushad + been + V-ing過去某時間前一直在進行for, since, by the time

考核要點總結 ✅

  1. Simple Past 用於已結束的過去動作，常與 yesterday / ago / last week 連用。
  2. Past Continuous 強調過去某刻正在進行；常見 when + 簡單式（打斷）或 while + 進行式（同時）。
  3. Present Perfect 連接過去和現在；不能與明確的過去時間連用。
  4. Present Perfect Continuous 強調動作的持續性；只用於 action verbs。
  5. Past Perfect 用於「過去的過去」— 先發生的動作。
  6. Past Perfect Continuous 強調過去某時間之前一直在進行的動作。
⚠️ 重點提醒

Present Perfect 絕不能與明確的過去時間（yesterday, last week, ago）連用！這是考試必考陷阱！
