Unit 2: Talking about the Past — Practice Test

總分：100 分 | 建議時限：45 分鐘

本測驗涵蓋 Simple Past、Past Continuous、Present Perfect、Present Perfect Continuous、Past Perfect 及 Past Perfect Continuous。

Section A: Multiple Choice（選擇題）— 10 題（每題 2 分）

Choose the best answer to complete each sentence.

1. When I arrived at the party, everyone ______ already ______ home.

✅ 答案：B

2. She ______ Chinese for three years before she moved to Hong Kong.

✅ 答案：C

3. I ______ my homework yet. Can you give me five more minutes?

✅ 答案：A

4. While I ______ TV last night, the power suddenly went out.

✅ 答案：B

5. We ______ each other since we were in primary school.

✅ 答案：C

6. By the time the teacher arrived, the students ______ the classroom.

✅ 答案：C

7. My mother ______ dinner when I got home from school.

✅ 答案：D

8. I ______ never ______ to Japan. Is it nice?

✅ 答案：A

9. She looks exhausted because she ______ all day.

✅ 答案：C

10. We ______ tennis yesterday afternoon. It was fun!

✅ 答案：B

Section B: Fill in the Blanks（填空）— 10 題（每題 3 分）

Fill in the blanks with the correct form of the verb in brackets.

11. I _______________ (already / finish) my homework before my mother came home.

✅ 答案：had already finished

12. She _______________ (wait) for the bus for 30 minutes when it finally arrived.

✅ 答案：had been waiting

13. They _______________ (live) in this city since 2015.

✅ 答案：have lived || have been living

14. Tom _______________ (not / do) his homework last night because he was too tired.

✅ 答案：did not do || didn't do

15. While we _______________ (have) dinner, the telephone rang.

✅ 答案：were having

16. I _______________ (never / try) sushi before. Let's order some!

✅ 答案：have never tried

17. By the time we reached the cinema, the movie _______________ (already / start).

✅ 答案：had already started

18. She _______________ (study) English for five years, and she speaks very well.

✅ 答案：has been studying

19. The ground was wet. It _______________ (rain) all night.

✅ 答案：had been raining

20. My grandfather _______________ (work) as a teacher for 30 years before he retired.

✅ 答案：had worked || had been working

Section C: Error Correction（改錯）— 5 題（每題 4 分）

Each sentence has ONE grammar mistake. Write the corrected sentence.

21. I have seen a great movie yesterday with my friends.

✅ I saw a great movie yesterday with my friends.

22. She was knowing the answer but she didn't say anything.

✅ She knew the answer but she didn't say anything.

23. They have been living in Hong Kong since five years.

✅ They have been living in Hong Kong for five years.

24. When I arrived, she already left.

✅ When I arrived, she had already left.

25. He didn't went to school because he was sick.

✅ He didn't go to school because he was sick.

Section D: Sentence Rewriting（句子改寫）— 5 題（每題 6 分）

Rewrite the sentences according to the instructions.

26. I was sleeping. The earthquake happened. (Combine using Past Continuous + Simple Past)

✅ 答案：I was sleeping when the earthquake happened.

27. I started learning French three years ago and I still learn it now. (Rewrite using Present Perfect)

✅ 答案：I have learned French for three years. || I have been learning French for three years.

28. First, she finished her homework. Then, she watched TV. (Rewrite using Past Perfect)

✅ 答案：She had finished her homework before she watched TV.

29. I have ever tried Korean food. (Change to negative)

✅ 答案：I have never tried Korean food.

30. It started raining at 8 a.m. It is still raining now. (Rewrite using Present Perfect Continuous)

✅ 答案：It has been raining since 8 a.m.
