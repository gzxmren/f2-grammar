Unit 3: Talking about the Future — Practice Test

總分：100 分 | 建議時限：45 分鐘

Section A: Multiple Choice 10題（每題2分）

1. The train to Beijing ______ at 8:30 a.m. tomorrow.

✅ 答案：B

2. I promise I ______ you with your project this weekend.

✅ 答案：C

3. Look at those dark clouds! It ______ soon.

✅ 答案：D

4. We ______ dinner at a fancy restaurant tonight. I've already booked.

✅ 答案：B

5. By the time you arrive, I ______ all my packing.

✅ 答案：C

6. A: The phone is ringing! B: Don't worry, I ______ it.

✅ 答案：C

7. This time next week, we ______ on a beach in Thailand!

✅ 答案：B

8. If it ______ tomorrow, we will cancel the picnic.

✅ 答案：B

9. I ______ my doctor at 3 p.m. tomorrow. I have an appointment.

✅ 答案：A

10. ______ we go to the cinema this evening?

✅ 答案：D

Section B: Fill in 10題（每題3分）

11. The concert _______________ (start) at 7:30 p.m. sharp.

✅ 答案：starts

12. I _______________ (help) you carry those bags. They look heavy.

✅ 答案：will help

13. She _______________ (study) abroad next year. She has already applied.

✅ 答案：is going to study

14. According to the forecast, it _______________ (be) sunny tomorrow.

✅ 答案：will be

15. This time tomorrow, I _______________ (take) my final exam.

✅ 答案：will be taking

16. By the end of this year, my parents _______________ (live) here for 20 years.

✅ 答案：will have lived

17. A: What are your plans? B: I _______________ (travel) to Japan with my family.

✅ 答案：am going to travel || am travelling

18. The supermarket _______________ (open) at 9 a.m. and _______________ (close) at 10 p.m.

✅ 答案：opens; closes

19. I _______________ (finish) the report by the time the boss arrives.

✅ 答案：will have finished

20. Watch out! You _______________ (drop) that glass!

✅ 答案：are going to drop

Section C: Error Correction 5題（每題4分）

21. I am going to meeting my friends after school today.

✅ I am going to meet my friends after school today.

22. If it will rain tomorrow, we will stay at home.

✅ If it rains tomorrow, we will stay at home.

23. I will helping you with your homework if you need me.

✅ I will help you with your homework if you need me.

24. Look at that car! It will crash into the tree!

✅ Look at that car! It is going to crash into the tree!

25. The bus will leave at 8 a.m. tomorrow according to the timetable.

✅ The bus leaves at 8 a.m. tomorrow according to the timetable.

Section D: Sentence Rewriting 5題（每題6分）

26. I plan to visit my grandparents this weekend. (用 be going to)

✅ 答案：I am going to visit my grandparents this weekend.

27. At midnight tonight, I will sleep. (用 Future Continuous)

✅ 答案：At midnight tonight, I will be sleeping.

28. I will finish the project before next Friday. (用 Future Perfect)

✅ 答案：I will have finished the project by next Friday.

29. We have arranged to meet at the library at 3 p.m. (用 Present Continuous for future)

✅ 答案：We are meeting at the library at 3 p.m.

30. Do you want me to open the window? (用 Shall)

✅ 答案：Shall I open the window?
