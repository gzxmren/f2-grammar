Unit 4: The Passive — Practice Test

總分：100 分 | 建議時限：45 分鐘

Section A: MC 10題（每題2分）

1. English ______ in many countries around the world.

✅ 答案：B

2. The new hospital ______ at the moment. It will open next year.

✅ 答案：B

3. All the homework ______ by the students before the deadline.

✅ 答案：B

4. The famous painting ______ by a thief last night.

✅ 答案：C

5. I need to ______ my phone ______ because the screen is broken.

✅ 答案：B

6. A new bridge ______ across the river next year.

✅ 答案：B

7. The problem ______ yet. We are still working on it.

✅ 答案：B

8. Dinner ______ by my mother every evening.

✅ 答案：A

9. The car ______ when I arrived at the garage.

✅ 答案：C

10. The concert ______ to next month because of the typhoon.

✅ 答案：B

Section B: 主動改被動 10題（每題3分）

Rewrite from active to passive. Omit the agent unless necessary.

11. The chef prepares the salad. → The salad _______________

✅ 答案：is prepared

12. The students will finish the project. → The project _______________

✅ 答案：will be finished

13. Someone has stolen my bike. → My bike _______________

✅ 答案：has been stolen

14. The police caught the thief yesterday. → The thief _______________

✅ 答案：was caught yesterday

15. You must return the books by Friday. → The books _______________

✅ 答案：must be returned by Friday

16. The government is building a new airport. → A new airport _______________

✅ 答案：is being built

17. My mother had already made the cake. → The cake _______________

✅ 答案：had already been made

18. They are going to open a new shopping mall. → A new shopping mall _______________

✅ 答案：is going to be opened

19. Someone should clean the classroom. → The classroom _______________

✅ 答案：should be cleaned

20. The company has offered her a job. → She _______________

✅ 答案：has been offered a job

Section C: Error Correction 5題（每題4分）

21. The window broken by a ball yesterday.

✅ The window was broken by a ball yesterday.

22. The letter is send every morning.

✅ The letter is sent every morning.

23. This work must is finished by 5 p.m. today.

✅ This work must be finished by 5 p.m. today.

24. She had her hair cutted at the salon.

✅ She had her hair cut at the salon.

25. The accident was happened near my school.

✅ The accident happened near my school.

Section D: Sentence Rewriting 5題（每題6分）

26. Someone delivers the newspapers every morning. (用被動)

✅ 答案：The newspapers are delivered every morning.

27. A professional photographer took our wedding photos. (用 have sth done)

✅ 答案：We had our wedding photos taken by a professional photographer.

28. Shakespeare wrote Romeo and Juliet. (保留施動者)

✅ 答案：Romeo and Juliet was written by Shakespeare.

29. You can solve this problem easily. (用情態被動)

✅ 答案：This problem can be solved easily.

30. People believe that exercise is good for health. (用被動)

✅ 答案：Exercise is believed to be good for health.
