Unit 5: Conditional Sentences — Practice Test

滿分：100 分 | 建議時間：45 分鐘

Part 1: Multiple Choice 12題×3分=36分

1. If you ________ ice, it floats.

✅ 答案：B

2. If I ________ you, I would apologise immediately.

✅ 答案：C

3. If it rains tomorrow, we ________ the picnic.

✅ 答案：C

4. If she had studied medicine, she ________ a doctor now.

✅ 答案：B

5. I wish I ________ more careful in the last exam.

✅ 答案：D

6. If you ________ me earlier, I could have helped you.

✅ 答案：C

7. If you had eaten spoiled food, you ________ sick.

✅ 答案：C

8. If only I ________ how to swim when I was young!

✅ 答案：C

9. If he hadn't missed the bus, he ________ late for the interview.

✅ 答案：B

10. She wishes she ________ more time last week.

✅ 答案：C

11. If you mix red and blue, you ________ purple.

✅ 答案：B

12. If I ________ what you were going through, I would have been more understanding.

✅ 答案：C

Part 2: Fill in the Blanks 12題×3分=36分

13. If you ________ (heat) water to 100°C, it boils.

✅ 答案：heat

14. If I ________ (have) enough money, I will buy a new phone.

✅ 答案：have

15. If I ________ (be) you, I would accept the offer.

✅ 答案：were

16. If he ________ (not / waste) so much time, he would have finished on time.

✅ 答案：had not wasted || hadn't wasted

17. I wish I ________ (can) fly like a bird.

✅ 答案：could

18. If she ________ (study) harder last term, she would have got better grades.

✅ 答案：had studied

19. If you had told the truth, the teacher ________ (not / punish) you.

✅ 答案：would not have punished || wouldn't have punished

20. If only I ________ (not / forget) to bring my umbrella!

✅ 答案：had not forgotten || hadn't forgotten

21. If it ________ (rain) tomorrow, the match will be cancelled.

✅ 答案：rains

22. He wishes he ________ (not / spend) all his savings last year.

✅ 答案：had not spent || hadn't spent

23. If you had arrived five minutes earlier, you ________ (meet) the famous singer.

✅ 答案：would have met

24. If I ________ (know) her phone number, I would call her right now.

✅ 答案：knew

Part 3: Error Correction 5題×3分=15分

25. If I will see him, I will give him your message.

✅ If I see him, I will give him your message.

26. If I was you, I would not make the same mistake.

✅ If I were you, I would not make the same mistake.

27. If she had studied harder, she would passed the exam.

✅ If she had studied harder, she would have passed the exam.

28. I wish I am taller so that I can play basketball better.

✅ I wish I were taller so that I can play basketball better.

29. If you had told me about the party, I could come.

✅ If you had told me about the party, I could have come.

Part 4: Sentence Rewriting 8題（答案見下方）

30. I didn't take the medicine, so I didn't get better quickly. (改Type3)

✅ 答案：If I had taken the medicine, I would have got better quickly.

31. He didn't study, and he failed the test. (用 wish)

✅ 答案：He wishes he had studied for the test.

32. She is not tall enough to reach the shelf. (用 If only)

✅ 答案：If only she were taller!

33. I don't have a car, so I cannot drive you. (改Type2)

✅ 答案：If I had a car, I would drive you to the airport.

34. They didn't save money, now they can't buy a house. (用 wish)

✅ 答案：They wish they had saved money.

35. We didn't leave early, so we missed the train. (Type3 + could have)

✅ 答案：If we had left early, we could have caught the train.

36. I want to become a pilot, but I have poor eyesight. (If only)

✅ 答案：If only I had better eyesight!

37. The alarm didn't ring, so I overslept and was late. (Type3 + might have)

✅ 答案：If the alarm had rung, I might not have been late for school.

Part 5: Matching 3題×2分=6分

38. If I had known you were coming, … (配對) → B

✅ 答案：B

39. If you heat ice, … (配對) → C

✅ 答案：C

40. If I had checked the weather forecast, … (配對) → A

✅ 答案：A
