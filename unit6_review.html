Unit 6: Review Test 1 — Units 1-5 綜合複習

總分：100 分 | 建議時限：60 分鐘

Section A: Multiple Choice 15題（每題2分=30分）

1. Water ______ at 100 degrees Celsius.

✅ 答案：B

2. Look! The children ______ basketball.

✅ 答案：C

3. I ______ a headache. Can I take a rest?

✅ 答案：B

4. When I arrived, everyone ______ already ______.

✅ 答案：B

5. By the time you get home, I ______ dinner ready.

✅ 答案：A

6. If I ______ you, I would accept the offer.

✅ 答案：C

7. English ______ in many countries.

✅ 答案：B

8. I wish I ______ more time to prepare for the exam.

✅ 答案：B

9. She ______ Chinese for three years. She speaks it quite well.

✅ 答案：C

10. If it rains tomorrow, we ______ the picnic.

✅ 答案：C

11. The chef ______ the soup at the moment.

✅ 答案：B

12. A new hospital ______ near my house next year.

✅ 答案：B

13. I ______ never ______ to Disneyland.

✅ 答案：A

14. If she had studied harder, she ______ the exam.

✅ 答案：C

15. We usually ______ to the cinema but this week we ______ at home.

✅ 答案：B

Section B: Fill in 10題（每題3分=30分）

16. Please be quiet! I _______________ (try) to concentrate.

✅ 答案：am trying

17. She _______________ (already / finish) her homework before her mother came home.

✅ 答案：had already finished

18. If I _______________ (be) rich, I would travel around the world.

✅ 答案：were

19. The new road _______________ (build) at the moment.

✅ 答案：is being built

20. I _______________ (meet) my friends for dinner tonight. We have booked.

✅ 答案：am meeting

21. By the end of this year, I _______________ (live) here for five years.

✅ 答案：will have lived

22. If you _______________ (heat) ice, it melts.

✅ 答案：heat

23. She _______________ (not / have) a car, so she takes the bus.

✅ 答案：does not have || doesn't have

24. The report _______________ (must / finish) before Friday.

✅ 答案：must be finished

25. If I _______________ (know) about the party, I would have gone.

✅ 答案：had known

Section C: Error Correction 5題（每題4分=20分）

26. I am wanting to buy a present.

✅ I want to buy a present.

27. I have seen a great movie yesterday.

✅ I saw a great movie yesterday.

28. If I will have time, I will help you.

✅ If I have time, I will help you.

29. The window broken by a ball yesterday.

✅ The window was broken by a ball yesterday.

30. Look at those clouds! It will rain soon.

✅ Look at those clouds! It is going to rain soon.

Section D: Sentence Rewriting 5題（每題4分=20分）

31. The chef cooks dinner every evening. (改被動)

✅ 答案：Dinner is cooked by the chef every evening.

32. First she finished homework, then she went out. (用Past Perfect)

✅ 答案：She had finished her homework before she went out.

33. I don't have a car, so I can't drive to work. (改Type2條件句)

✅ 答案：If I had a car, I would drive to work.

34. We have arranged to visit our grandparents this Sunday. (用Present Continuous)

✅ 答案：We are visiting our grandparents this Sunday.

35. I didn't study hard for the exam, so I failed. (用wish)

✅ 答案：I wish I had studied hard for the exam.
