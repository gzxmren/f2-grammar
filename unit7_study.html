Unit 7: Verb + to-infinitive and Verb + Gerund

英文中，某些動詞後面可以接 to-infinitive（不定式） 或 gerund（動名詞，即 V-ing），但含義可能相似或完全不同。

類別 1：意義相近的動詞

動詞to-infinitivegerund備註
beginbegan to rainbegan raining意思相同
startstarted to crystarted crying意思相同
continuecontinued to talkcontinued talking意思相同
hatehate to get uphate getting up口語中gerund更常見
like / lovelikes to readlikes reading意思相同
preferprefer to drink teaprefer drinking tea意思相同
⚠️ 特殊規則

進行式中用 to-inf：✗ It is beginning raining. → ✅ It is beginning to rain.
+ know/realize/understand 用 to-inf：✗ begin realizing → ✅ begin to realize

類別 2：意義改變的動詞 ⭐ 重點！

動詞+ to-infinitive+ gerund
remember記得要去做（未做）記得做過（已做）
forget忘記去做（未做）忘記做過（已做）
stop停下為了去做停止正在做的事
try努力去做試試某方法

記憶口訣

  • to-do → 未做；doing → 已做
  • stop doing = 不做；stop to do = 停下為了做
  • try to do = 努力嘗試；try doing = 試試看

go + gerund 常見搭配

短語中文
go shopping去購物
go swimming去游泳
go fishing去釣魚
go hiking去遠足
go camping去露營
go running去跑步
go skiing去滑雪
go dancing去跳舞
go sightseeing去觀光
go jogging去慢跑
go bowling去打保齡球
go skating去溜冰

易錯點提醒

錯誤 ❌正確 ✅解析
I remember to lock the door, but I can't find my keys.I remember locking the door…已鎖了門（已做），用 gerund
Please remember locking the door before you leave.Please remember to lock…還沒鎖（未做），用 to-inf
He stopped to smoke because the doctor told him to quit.He stopped smoking…戒煙（停止抽煙）
It is beginning raining.It is beginning to rain.進行式中用 to-inf
