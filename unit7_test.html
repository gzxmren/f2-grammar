Unit 7: Verb + to-infinitive and Gerund — Practice Test

總分：40 分 | 建議時間：45 分鐘

Section A: MC 12題（每題1分）

1. Please remember _____ the windows before you leave.

✅ 答案：B

2. I will never forget _____ my favourite singer last year.

✅ 答案：B

3. The doctor advised me to stop _____ fast food.

✅ 答案：C

4. She was tired, so she stopped _____ a short rest.

✅ 答案：B

5. If the computer doesn't work, try _____ it off and on.

✅ 答案：B

6. He tried his best _____ the heavy box.

✅ 答案：C

7. It is beginning _____ dark outside.

✅ 答案：B

8. Did you remember _____ your grandmother? She said you didn't call.

✅ 答案：A

9. My father loves _____ golf at weekends.

✅ 答案：D

10. Let's go _____ at the new shopping mall!

✅ 答案：C

11. He began _____ how much his parents had done only when he became a father.

✅ 答案：C

12. I regret _____ you that your application has been rejected.

✅ 答案：B

Section B: Fill in 12題（每題1分）

13. She enjoys ______________ (swim) in the sea.

✅ 答案：swimming

14. I have decided ______________ (study) abroad.

✅ 答案：to study

15. He forgot ______________ (lock) the car door, so thieves stole his bag.

✅ 答案：to lock

16. Do you remember ______________ (meet) me at the library last Monday?

✅ 答案：meeting

17. My mother suggested ______________ (go) to the beach.

✅ 答案：going

18. The teacher continued ______________ (explain) the grammar rules.

✅ 答案：to explain || explaining

19. When I'm stressed, I find that ______________ (listen) to music helps.

✅ 答案：listening

20. Please don't forget ______________ (bring) your calculator to the exam.

✅ 答案：to bring

21. He stopped ______________ (tie) his shoelaces when he saw his friend.

✅ 答案：to tie

22. I tried ______________ (study) with music on, but I couldn't concentrate.

✅ 答案：studying

23. She will never forget ______________ (visit) the Great Wall when she was ten.

✅ 答案：visiting

24. The weather is starting ______________ (get) colder.

✅ 答案：to get

Section C: Error Correction 6題（每題2分）

25. I remembered locking the door before leaving. Actually, I forgot locking it.

✅ I forgot to lock it.

26. When she heard the news, she stopped to cry and wiped away her tears.

✅ She stopped crying and wiped away her tears.

27. They continued playing the game despite the rain.

✅ No error

28. It's beginning getting dark, so we'd better hurry home.

✅ It's beginning to get dark, so we'd better hurry home.

29. He tried to use the new software but found it too complicated.

✅ No error

30. I regret telling you that you have failed the test.

✅ I regret to tell you that you have failed the test.

Section D: Sentence Rewriting 5題（每題2分）

31. "Need to buy milk on my way home" (用 remember)

✅ 答案：remember to buy some milk on her way home

32. He tried hard to open the window, but it was stuck. (用 try)

✅ 答案：tried to open the window, but it was stuck

33. She takes a break from working every two hours to stretch. (用 stop)

✅ 答案：stops to stretch her legs every two hours

34. I called mother yesterday because I didn't want to forget. (用 remember)

✅ 答案：remembered to call my mother yesterday

35. We went to the beach and swam in the sea. (用 go)

✅ 答案：went swimming in the sea
