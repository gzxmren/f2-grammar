Unit 8: Adjective + to-infinitive — Practice Test

總分：100 分 | 建議時限：40 分鐘

Section A: MC 10題（每題2分）

1. It is important ______ for the exam.

✅ 答案：C

2. She is ______ to drive a car. She is only 15.

✅ 答案：A

3. I am very happy ______ you here today.

✅ 答案：C

4. He is ______ to solve the problem by himself.

✅ 答案：B

5. It was very kind ______ me with my luggage.

✅ 答案：B

6. The box is ______ for me to lift. Can you help?

✅ 答案：A

7. ______ is difficult to learn a new language.

✅ 答案：C

8. You are ______ to win the competition!

✅ 答案：B

9. The water is ______ to swim in. Let's go!

✅ 答案：B

10. It was very rude ______ during the meeting.

✅ 答案：A

Section B: Fill in 10題（每題3分）

11. It ______________________________ (important / wear) a seatbelt.

✅ 答案：is important to wear

12. She ______________________________ (kind / lend) me her notes.

✅ 答案：was kind to lend

13. The coffee is ______________________________ (hot / drink).

✅ 答案：too hot to drink

14. He is ______________________________ (tall / reach) the top shelf.

✅ 答案：tall enough to reach

15. ______________ is dangerous ______________ (swim) alone in the sea.

✅ 答案：It; to swim

16. We were ______________________________ (surprised / hear) the news.

✅ 答案：surprised to hear

17. The problem is ______________________________ (difficult / solve) in 5 min.

✅ 答案：too difficult to solve

18. Are you ______________________________ (old / vote) in the next election?

✅ 答案：old enough to vote

19. It was very ______________________________ (silly / him / believe) that story.

✅ 答案：silly of him to believe

20. I am ______________________________ (tired / go) out tonight.

✅ 答案：too tired to go

Section C: Error Correction 5題（每題4分）

21. It is important studying hard for the exam.

✅ It is important to study hard for the exam.

22. He is enough old to drive a car.

✅ He is old enough to drive a car.

23. I am very happy help you with your homework.

✅ I am very happy to help you with your homework.

24. The bag is too heavy that I cannot carry it.

✅ The bag is too heavy to carry.

25. It was very kind for him to donate money.

✅ It was very kind of him to donate money.

Section D: Sentence Rewriting 5題（每題6分）

26. Learning a new language is fun. (用 It + be + adj)

✅ 答案：It is fun to learn a new language.

27. She is so young that she cannot watch this movie. (用 too...to)

✅ 答案：She is too young to watch this movie.

28. He is very strong. He can carry the piano. (用 adj + enough)

✅ 答案：He is strong enough to carry the piano by himself.

29. It was generous of her to donate all that money. (用 S + be + adj)

✅ 答案：She was generous to donate all that money.

30. I am so busy that I cannot take a holiday this year. (用 too...to)

✅ 答案：I am too busy to take a holiday this year.
