Unit 9: To-infinitives & -ing — Practice Test

總分：100分 | 建議時限：40分鐘

Section A: Multiple Choice 10題（每題3分=30分）

1. Would you like something ______?

✅ 答案：C

2. She borrowed a novel ______ during the holidays.

✅ 答案：B

3. I need a chair ______.

✅ 答案：B

4. We are pleased ______ the winner.

✅ 答案：C

5. He is a pleasant person ______.

✅ 答案：B

6. I still have a lot of work ______.

✅ 答案：C

7. She has a decision ______.

✅ 答案：C

8. He is looking for a restaurant ______.

✅ 答案：B

9. There is nothing ______.

✅ 答案：B

10. I need someone ______.

✅ 答案：B

Section B: Fill in 10題（每題3分=30分）

11. Would you like something _______________ (drink)?

✅ 答案：to drink

12. She needs a hotel _______________ (stay at).

✅ 答案：to stay at

13. I am pleased _______________ (announce) the winner.

✅ 答案：to announce

14. She has many skills _______________ (apply) to the project.

✅ 答案：to apply

15. There is nothing _______________ (worry about).

✅ 答案：to worry about

16. I need a pen _______________ (write with).

✅ 答案：to write with

17. She still has two more reports _______________ (write).

✅ 答案：to write

18. He is a nice person _______________ (work with).

✅ 答案：to work with

19. I was surprised _______________ (see) him there.

✅ 答案：to see

20. We are happy _______________ (help) you.

✅ 答案：to help

Section C: Error Correction 5題（每題4分=20分）

21. She borrowed a novel to read it during the holidays.

✅ She borrowed a novel to read during the holidays.

22. I need a knife to cut it with.

✅ I need a knife to cut with.

23. He is looking for a restaurant to eat.

✅ He is looking for a restaurant to eat at.

24. Would you like something for drink?

✅ Would you like something to drink?

25. I still have a lot of work for doing.

✅ I still have a lot of work to do.

Section D: Sentence Rewriting 5題（每題4分=20分）

26. I want to drink something. → I want something _______________

✅ 答案：to drink

27. He needs a hotel. He can stay at it. → He needs a hotel _______________

✅ 答案：to stay at

28. She is happy. She helps others. → She is happy _______________

✅ 答案：to help others

29. I have work. I must do it. → I have work _______________

✅ 答案：to do

30. He is a good person. We can work with him. → He is a good person _______________

✅ 答案：to work with
